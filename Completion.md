## Moves

- Moves without secondary effect
    - Cut
    - Drill Peck
    - Earthquake
    - Egg Bomb
    - Gust
    - Horn Attack
    - Mega Kick
    - Peck
    - Pound
    - Rock Slide
    - Rock Throw
    - Scratch
    - Slam
    - Tackle
    - Tri Attack
    - Vice Grip
    - Vine Whip
    - Water Gun
    - Wing Attack

- Moves with high critical
    - Karate Chop
    - Razor Leaf
    - Slash

- Moves with low priorities
    - Counter (-1)

- Moves with high priorities
    - Quick Attack (+1)

- Moves with specific damage
    - Dragon Rage (40)
    - Seismic Toss (User level)
    - Sonic Boom (20)
    - Super Fang (50% remaining HP)

- Moves hitting N times in one turn
    - Barrage (2-5)
    - Double Kick (2-2)
    - Double Slap (2-5)
    - Fury Attack (2-5)
    - Fury Swipes (2-5)
    - Pin Missile (2-5)
    - Twineedle (2-2)

- Moves hitting N turns consecutively and ending with confusion
    - Petal Dance (3-4)
    - Thrash (3-4)

- Moves draining HP
    - Absorb
    - Leech Life
    - Mega Drain

- Moves inflicting statuses
    - Burn
        - Ember
        - Flamethrower
    - Confusion
        - Confuse Ray
        - Confusion
        - Psybeam
        - Supersonic
    - Flinch
        - Bite
        - Bone Club
        - Headbutt
        - Hyper Fang
        - Low Kick
    - Freeze
        - Ice Beam
    - Paralysis
        - Body Slam (except Normal types)
        - Glare
        - Stun Spore
        - Thunderbolt  (except Electric types)
        - Thunder Shock (except Electric types)
        - Thunder Wave
    - Poison
        - Poison Gas
        - Poison Powder
        - Poison Sting
        - Smog
        - Twineedle (after last hit)
    - Seed
        - Leech Seed
    - Sleep
        - Hypnosis
        - Sing
        - Sleep Powder

- Moves dropping target stats by one stage
    - Acid (defense)
    - Bubble (speed)
    - Constrict (speed)
    - Growl (attack)
    - Kinesis (accuracy)
    - Leer (defense)
    - Psychic (special)
    - Sand Attack (accuracy)
    - String Shot (speed)
    - Tail Whip (defense)

- Moves dropping target stats by two stages
    - Screech (defense)

- Moves raising user stats by one stage
    - Defense Curl (defense)
    - Double Team (evasion)
    - Growth (special)
    - Harden (defense)
    - Minimize (evasion)
    - Sharpen (attack)
    - Withdraw (defense)

- Moves raising user stats by two stages
    - Agility (speed)
    - Barrier (defense)
    - Swords Dance (attack)

- Moves forcing Pokemon to switch out (no effect in trainer battles in generation I)
    - Roar
    - Whirlwind

- Moves binding the opponent
    - Bind
    - Wrap

- Moves restoring user HP
    - Recover

- Moves with recoil damage
    - Struggle (50%)
    - Submission (25%)
    - Take Down (25%)

- Moves fainting user 
    - Self-destruct

- Moves doing one hit KO on slower opponents
    - Guillotine
    - Horn Drill

- Moves dividing target defense during damage calculation
    - Self-destruct (by 2)

- Moves dropping money
    - Payday

- Moves impossible to miss by regular accuracy checks
    - Swift

- Moves taking several turns
    - Dig (underground turn 1, attack turn 2)
    - Fly (in the air turn1, attack turn 2)
    - Razor Wind (making whirlwind turn 1, attack turn 2)
    - Skull Bash (lowering head turn 1, attack turn 2)

- Moves with effects on the map
    - Cut
    - Dig
    - Flash
    - Teleport

- Barrier moves
    - Light Screen (special attacks)
    - Reflect (physical attacks)

- Special moves
    - Bide (activate turn 1, wait 1-2 turns, and unleash last turn double damage taken while waiting)
    - Counter (send back twice damage if hit by normal/fighting moves)
    - Disable (disable a random move from the enemy)
    - Focus Energy (multiply critical chances by 4 - bug fixed compared to original RBY games which were dividing by 4 instead)
    - Mirror Move (replicate last attack from the opponent)
    - Splash (no effect at all)
    - Struggle (launched with 0pp)
    - Substitute (create substitute absorbing damage and canceling move effects against 25% of the user hp)
